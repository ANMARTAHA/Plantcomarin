Section 01

Overview

The definitions and facts worth knowing cold — not the full lecture. Read these before anything else.

Origin

What they are

Coumarins and chromones are benzopyrone derivatives, widely distributed secondary metabolites in higher plants, produced mainly through the phenylpropanoid (shikimic acid) pathway.

Coumarin

Coumarin core

Coumarins are 1,2-benzopyrones (α-pyrone) — a benzene ring fused to an α-pyrone ring, carbonyl at position 2.

Chromone

Chromone core

Chromones are 1,4-benzopyrones (γ-pyrone) — isomeric with coumarins, but the carbonyl sits at position 4 instead of 2.

Coumarin subclasses

  • Simple coumarins — umbelliferone, esculetin, scopoletin
  • Furanocoumarins — linear (psoralens: psoralen, bergapten, xanthotoxin) and angular (angelicins)
  • Pyranocoumarins
  • Coumarin glycosides — aesculin
  • Bicoumarins — dicoumarol
  • Synthetic 4-hydroxycoumarins — warfarin, acenocoumarol

Chromone groups

  • Simple chromones — chromone, 3-hydroxy/dihydroxychromone
  • Chromone derivatives — khellin, visnagin, cromolyn sodium, noreugenin
  • Xanthones — chromone fused with a phenyl ring (mangiferin, alpha-mangostin, gentisin)
  • Chromone C-glycosides — aloin A/B, aloesin, cassiaside

C-glycosides vs O-glycosides

Chromone C-glycosides are joined by a C–C bond between sugar and aglycone, so they resist hydrolysis. O-glycosides (like aesculin) use a C–O bond and hydrolyse more readily.

Landmark drug

Warfarin & dicoumarol

Warfarin was inspired by dicoumarol, which forms when coumarin in sweet clover is oxidised by fungi (Aspergillus, Penicillium). Both inhibit vitamin K epoxide reductase (VKORC1).

Biosynthesis, in one line

Shikimic acid pathway → phenylalanine → (PAL) cinnamate → p-coumarate / caffeate / ferulate → CoA esters → ortho-hydroxylation → spontaneous cis-lactonisation → coumarin / umbelliferone / esculetin / scopoletin.

Test

Identifying coumarins

  • UV 365 nm — blue fluorescence
  • Alkali test — greenish-blue (lactone ring opens)
  • FeCl₃ test — green (phenolic coumarins)
  • Ammonia vapour — enhances fluorescence of hydroxycoumarins
Test

Identifying chromones

  • Bontrager reaction — anthraquinone-related derivatives
  • UV fluorescence — yellow or green
  • TLC — distinct Rf in non-polar to moderately polar systems

Pharmacology — coumarins

  • Anticoagulant — dicoumarol, warfarin
  • Anti-inflammatory — esculetin, aescin
  • Photosensitising — psoralens (PUVA)
  • Antioxidant & antimicrobial

Pharmacology — chromones

  • Bronchodilator — khellin
  • Antispasmodic — chromone derivatives
  • Anti-inflammatory — khellin analogues
  • Laxative — chromone C-glycosides in aloe (aloin)

Clinical applications

  • PUVA therapy — psoralens + UVA, for psoriasis/vitiligo
  • Venous insufficiency — aescin from horse chestnut
  • Asthma therapy precursor — khellin
  • GI formulations — aloe chromone glycosides

Plant sources, quick list

  • Aesculus hippocastanum — aesculin, aesculetin
  • Melilotus officinalis — coumarin, dicoumarol
  • Ammi majus — psoralen, xanthotoxin
  • Citrus bergamia — bergapten, bergamottin
  • Ammi visnaga — khellin, visnagin
